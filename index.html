Part 1: moving without a petrol tank

Transport uses 25% of the nation's energy, and private cars account for 61.68% of that. The policy works on two fronts: electrify the cars people already drive, and build a public network good enough to leave the car at home.

Will the 2040 deadline deliver the 2050 target?

Ending new combustion sales in 2040 does not empty the roads overnight. Old cars keep driving until they are retired. The NEP targets a 90% cut in private combustion vehicles by 2050, so combustion cars must fall to 10% of the fleet or less. Adjust the assumptions and see whether it gets there.

Sales share rises in a straight line from 2025 to 2030, then from 2030 to 100% in 2040. The report gives no starting figures, so these are placeholders you can change.

The benchmark: 21.7% of journeys to work

The Weinland in Switzerland is spread over 175 square kilometres of small villages, yet 21.7% of people there get to work by public transport. The NEP sets 21.7% as the minimum goal for rural rail by 2043. Drag to see what a share looks like.
